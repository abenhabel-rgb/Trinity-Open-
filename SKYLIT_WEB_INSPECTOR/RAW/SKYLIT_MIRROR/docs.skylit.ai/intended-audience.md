## Who is Heatseeker For?

Heatseeker is designed for serious traders who want a greater understanding of:

* Why the markets move the way they do
* Identifying areas of interest and proactively acting directly on reversals instead of reacting to them
* Improving timing and conviction by aligning technical analysis with dealer positioning
* Build a systematic, disciplined and objective approach to reading the market instead of trading off emotion or speculation

It is especially powerful for traders of **SPX, SPY, QQQ, /ES, /NQ**, and other **high-liquidity tickers**, but its principles apply across the broader market.

### What Heatseeker is NOT

* It is **NOT** a shortcut to instant profitability
* **NOT** a guaranteed win, every single trade carries risk
* **NOT** a replacement for your own strategy and discipline
* **NOT** a signal bot, it is a tool meant to complement technical analysis
